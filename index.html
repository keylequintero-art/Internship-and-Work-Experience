Internship & Work experience

The Intern Project

    Grade: 10-12
    Format: In person/Hybrid
    Requirements:Must attend an LA county highschool
    Summary: Provides LA county highschoolers with internship experiences in fields such as business, healthcare government, Technology, Sports and entertainment gaining professional experience and career skills

La County Bloomberg Arts Internship

    Grade:Rising 12th
    Format: In person
    Requirements: For LA county public school seniors
    Summary: An arts focused summer internship where students work with cultural organizations and receive mentorship developing portfolios as well

Exploring the Arts

    Grade: 11
    Format: Hybrid/ In person
    Requirements: 11th graders at participating ETA partner schools, good academic standing and commitment
    Summary: Gives students paid experience working with arts and cultural organizations while providing mentorship and professional development.

Turner- UCLA Allied Health Internship

    Grade: 11-12 & Recent Graduates
    Format: In person
    Requirements: Designed for juniors seniors and eligible recent graduates application required.
    Summary: Introduces students to health care and allied health careers through career exploration education and professional experiences.

LAUSD Summer Internship Program

    Grade: Rising 12th
    Format: In person/ Hybrid
    Requirements: Meet lausd eligibility requirements and be eligible for a work permit
    Summary: Gives Lausd students summer work experience with employers and LAUSD departments while developing workplace and career skills.
